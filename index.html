CONDITION CHARACTER EDITOR

Drag any bounding box or health bar to reposition it.

Click a body-part box to open its pixel editor.

Click a health bar to select or disable arrow cap end.

Right Click a body-part to change its damage state.

Every health bar has coordinate boxes for more precise placement.

Every body part has bounding box size customization (up to 100x100).

In the pixel editor: left-click paints the selected shade, right-click erases to transparent. Click-drag to paint continuously.

Connect and upload to upload the .char file to your Pip-Boy.

Connect and edit to edit a .char file on your Pip-Boy.

Export to download the .char file to your computer.

Import to import a .char file for editing.
